## Human prompt-review rubric

- Pre-registered the public-safe paired-review rubric before opening any
  Prompt v2 raw response. It separates primary-concept retention, the targeted
  unsupported-detail categories, excessive `unknown`, and repetition handling
  from deterministic evidence.
- The four-case cohort is too small for an invented percentage recall score.
  The rubric instead records every model/case where `unknown` suppresses a
  human-confirmed primary visible concept and publishes only aggregate counts
  and qualitative conclusions.
